# Hailstone's own flora rules (assets/zone_flora.h, docs/graphics.md "Zone flora"). The jungles (Emerald Jungle,
# Trakanon's Teeth, Firiona Vie...): the lawn with Arcstone's jungle ferns (arcstone.eqg) and the revamped
# Feerrott's bushes (feerrott2.eqg).
