# Hailstone's own flora rules (assets/zone_flora.h, docs/graphics.md "Zone flora"). The swamps (Innothule, the Swamp
# of No Hope): the lawn with the revamped Innothule's ferns (innothuleb.eqg) and a few of the Moors' cattails
# (moors.eqg).
donor innothuleb.eqg
donor moors.eqg
max_slope 45
scale 0.9 1.3
include bare
# Ground named as grass (jungle floors too): its olive and dark grass as well, only its paths and bare patches not.
ground *jung* 1.07 +greenish lawn flora_a:1 flora_e:1 flora_f:0.6 swampgrass:0.6 cattails:0.12
ground *grass* 1.07 +greenish lawn flora_a:1 flora_e:1 flora_f:0.6 swampgrass:0.6 cattails:0.12
ground *gras* 1.07 +greenish lawn flora_a:1 flora_e:1 flora_f:0.6 swampgrass:0.6 cattails:0.12
include stone
# Anything else that is clearly green.
ground * 1.07 +green lawn flora_a:1 flora_e:1 flora_f:0.6 swampgrass:0.6 cattails:0.12
